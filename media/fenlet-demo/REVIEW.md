# Desktop demo review

The story is a single workspace transformation: nine overlapping windows,
Arrange windows, an editable review, confirmation, and five organized panes.
Two background windows minimize and two clutter windows close. The final
layout and counts come from the real planner; its rectangles are checked for
overlap before rendering. The native control images and cursor targets come
from the actual SwiftUI widget and Accessibility bounds.

The desktop, documents, code, and conversations are fictional illustrations.
The 12-second silent loop suits README autoplay. Window movement is paced
to explain the workflow. The README identifies the illustration and timing.

The opening, review, transition, and completed workspace are inspected as
rendered frames, including a phone-size sheet. Headline, supporting copy,
display labels, results, and repository address use at least 28-pixel type.
The widget is enlarged to twice its native size and cropped around its
preview and confirmation controls.

Incidental document and window-chrome text is marked as illustration texture
for layout audits: the opening intentionally shows windows covering each
other, and app content clips at its viewport. Device bezel and native-image
cropping are also intentional. Geometry and reading copy remain audited.
Repeated-image warnings refer to the same static macOS icons and Fenlet mark
appearing in several places; they are not independently timed media.

The weakest detail is app body text at phone width. The workspace change,
headline, and results carry the story there; the linked MP4 provides the
full-resolution view. Sound is intentionally absent.
